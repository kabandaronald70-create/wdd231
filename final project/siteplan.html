Site Plan: Highway Secondary School Kiganda

WDD 231 — Web Frontend Development I | Kabanda Ronald

Site Name

Highway Secondary School Kiganda

Reason: This name clearly identifies the school's specific location (Kiganda Town Council, Kassanda District, along the Mubende-Highway Road in Uganda). It establishes a formal, credible, and easily searchable online identity that distinguishes the school from others in the region.

Optional domain availability: highwaysecondarykiganda.sc.ug

Site Purpose

The website serves as the official online information portal for Highway Secondary School Kiganda. It introduces the school's mission, vision, and values; provides a comprehensive listing of academic departments and subjects offered at both O-Level and A-Level; and offers a direct channel for parents and prospective students to submit inquiries and book admission appointments.

Scenarios (Target Audience Questions)

  1. What academic programs and specific subjects does Highway Secondary School Kiganda offer at both O-Level and A-Level?

    A parent or prospective student wants to verify if the school offers the right combination of sciences or humanities before visiting.

  2. How can I contact the school administration or schedule an admission appointment for my child?

    A busy parent needs the school's physical address, phone number, email, and a simple online form to book an admission inquiry meeting without making an unnecessary trip.

Color Scheme

Primary Green #1A4D2E

Used for main headings, navigation bar background, and primary call-to-action buttons.

Cream #FDF5E6

Used as the main background color for the page to provide warmth and high readability.

Chocolate #8B4513

Used for accent borders, section dividers, and secondary highlight elements.

Dark Grey #333333

Used for body text and footer text to ensure maximum contrast and legibility.

Contrast check passed: White on Green (4.6:1), Black on Cream (14:1), Chocolate on Cream (7:1).

Typography

  • Merriweather (Serif)

    Applied to all headings (<h1> to <h6>). This classic, academic serif font reflects the institution's formal and trustworthy educational identity.

  • Open Sans (Sans-Serif)

    Applied to all body text, paragraphs, and navigation links. This clean, highly readable font ensures a modern and user-friendly experience on all screen sizes.
