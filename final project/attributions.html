Resource Attributions

This page acknowledges the sources and resources used in creating the Highway Secondary School Kiganda website.

Content Sources

  • School Information: Based on the proposal submitted by Kabanda Ronald for Highway Secondary School Kiganda, Kiganda Town Council, Kassanda District, Uganda.
  • Academic Programs: Compiled from information provided by the school administration and curriculum guidelines from the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB).
  • School Statistics: Representative data reflecting typical Highway Secondary School metrics.

Design Resources

  • Color Scheme: Custom designed with Green (#1A4D2E), Cream (#FDF5E6), Chocolate (#8B4513), and Dark Grey (#333333). Inspired by the school's natural environment and institutional identity.
  • Typography: Merriweather (Serif) and Open Sans (Sans-Serif) provided by Google Fonts.
  • Icons: Department icons are Unicode symbols (🔬📖🎨🌍📊💻📐).

Technical Resources

  • Code: Built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES Modules). No third-party frameworks or libraries were used.
  • Validation: Tested with W3C Markup Validator, WAVE Accessibility Tool, and Chrome DevTools Lighthouse.
  • AI Assistance: An AI assistant (OpenAI ChatGPT) was used to help outline structure and refine code organization. All code was reviewed, tested, and verified by the author.
